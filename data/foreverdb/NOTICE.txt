ForeverDB - where each part of this addon comes from, and under which terms
=========================================================================

The licence is in LICENSE.txt (GPL-3.0 with an attribution term) and the GPL's
full text in COPYING.txt. In short: you may reuse this addon and its data, but
keep the notices in the files, and credit "Data from ForeverDB.net
(https://foreverdb.net)" where your work lists its credits and sources.

1. ForeverDB.net's code
   Every file outside Data/ (*.lua, *.xml, *.toc) and Media/ (images).
   Copyright (C) 2026 ForeverDB.net. GPL-3.0 with the attribution term.

2. ForeverDB.net's data (Data/*.lua)
   Every data file starts with the line
     -- Data: ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net), build <client build>. ...
   The data is labelled by where it comes from, in the files and in the addon:
   - Seen in Forever: what ForeverDB players' addons recorded in WoW Forever and
     uploaded to ForeverDB.net: drop rates measured over players' kills, where
     items dropped and where players looted them, vendors, their stock and their
     Merchant's Favor costs, flight times players timed, new quests and where
     their givers stand, disenchanting results, items players revealed, rare
     spawns, gathering spots, players' quest notes. This exists only because
     ForeverDB players recorded it. Copyright (C) 2026 ForeverDB.net.
   - ForeverDB.net's own work: gear scoring and stat weights, best-in-slot
     lists, the quest route planner's numbers, walking routes, guides, tips and
     texts. Copyright (C) 2026 ForeverDB.net.
   - The WoW Forever client: names and numbers read from the game's own tables
     (items, spells, quests, maps, talents, flight paths). These are Blizzard
     Entertainment's; ForeverDB.net claims nothing in them beyond its selection
     and arrangement.
   - Classic and community data: see 3 and 8 to 12 (QuestieDB, other players'
     drop counts, AllTheThings, Shortest Path Forever, VMaNGOS).
   Both copyrighted parts above are under GPL-3.0 with the attribution term in
   LICENSE.txt.

4. Files the ForeverDB Uploader writes on your PC
   Data/Helper.lua, Data/Reserves.lua, Data/Live.lua and Data/UploadAck.lua ship
   empty; the ForeverDB Uploader app fills them with your own status, your
   guilds' soft reserves and your realm's live data. They are not data to reuse.

8. Community data from QuestieDB (GPL-3.0)
   Upstream: https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB, the quest, creature, object and
   item database of the Questie addon (https://github.com/Questie/Questie),
   Copyright (C) the Questie and QuestieDB contributors, licensed under the GNU
   General Public License v3.0. Thank you to the Questie team and contributors.
   - Data/QuestQuestie.lua is derived from it as a whole: who gives and takes in
     quests Forever's own data has no giver for, where they and the quests'
     creatures and objects stand on Forever's maps, and quest levels, races and
     objectives. It wins over Classic data (CMaNGOS) where they differ and fills
     what is empty; it never replaces the Forever client's data or our players'
     recordings. Every row is
     labelled "Source: QuestieDB (GPL-3.0)" in the addon, and all of it can be
     switched off together in the registry (tools/data/source_registry.json).
   - We take facts (numbers, names, positions), not Questie's wording or logo.
     Questie's name and logo are not ours to use and are not used.
   - We use QuestieDB's own loader on its Forever data: the raw tables, the
     inherited fixes in Forever coordinates, the trace corrections and the
     hand-written Forever fixes. We leave out its "generated" layer, which was
     built from another website's pages.
   - Licence caveat, in QuestieDB's own words (LICENSE.md): its content was
     imported from Questie, which "historically never had a proper license"; a
     list of contributors has agreed to GPLv3, and the team is "alright with
     others forking the repository or copying code from it under the GPLv3
     terms, but we can not promise all contributors are". We ship it under
     GPL-3.0 on that basis and will remove any row on request.
   ForeverDB.net converts it with its own generator scripts (fetch_questie.py,
   questie_export.py, questie_fill.py, build_addon_quests.py, build_quests.py,
   build_zone_quests.py, build_quest_guides.py). The generated Lua file is the
   source we distribute; the generator scripts are available on request at
   https://foreverdb.net/contact.

11. Transport data from Shortest Path Forever (GPL-3.0-or-later)
   Upstream: https://github.com/cjber/shortest-path-forever, Copyright (C) its author
   and contributors, licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later. Thank
   you to its author.
   - Data/TravelCommunity.lua is derived from its boat and zeppelin timetables, portals
     and teleports, labelled "Source: Shortest Path Forever (GPL-3.0-or-later)". Only
     what ForeverDB's own travel data lacks is added, and the times keep their source
