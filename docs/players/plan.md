# Plan your next hours

**For:** players with a wow-stats page who follow a Polestar guide. **At the end:** you have a
plan for tonight's session and you know how to change it.

The planner turns "I have two hours" into a list: which guide steps, in what order, where to
stop in town, what to skip, and where to log out. It follows your Polestar guide at your own
pace, measured from your last sessions, not the route's.

The planner lives on your own page (the full view, behind your login), in the Character tab
under the character sheet. The friends' link and the live demo don't have it: it needs the
server. [A recorded session](../../site/pages/mcp-session.md) shows the same plan coming out of
an AI assistant.

## Make a plan

1. Pick how long you'll play: 1 to 6 hours.
2. Add what you don't want, in your own words: "no dungeons", "no flying back to town".
3. Click **Plan it**. It takes up to a minute.

## Read the plan

The plan comes in time blocks, each with what to do and why:

- **The steps**: your next Polestar guide steps, with the time each one should take you.
- **Checkpoints**: the level you should have at the end of each block. Behind at a
  checkpoint means the rest of the plan is late too: drop the next optional step.
- **The town stop**: when to train, sell and repair.
- **What to skip**: the steps that cost you more than they give at your level.
- **The dungeon branch**: if you find a group, the dungeon worth running instead, and what
  it does to the rest of the plan.
- **Riding**: the gold you have toward it and the hours of play still to go at your rate.
- **Log out**: where to stop, an inn or a city, so rested XP grows.

A section with nothing to say is left out.

## After you play

Next time you open the planner, it compares the last plan with what happened: "you needed
1.3× the planned time". Every
plan after that is scaled by how long the earlier ones really took you, so the plans get
closer to your pace the more you use them.

## With your AI

Your assistant can make the same plan and talk it through: "plan my next 2 hours, no
dungeons". It gets the same numbers and the server checks every number it writes back against
them, so a plan from your assistant can't invent a time or an XP figure.
[Ask about your character](../ai/asking.md).

## When there's no plan

"No plan data yet" means the character has no Polestar guide steps: it never followed a guide
with the addon on. Pick a guide in Polestar, play a few steps, `/reload`, and try again in
5 minutes.
