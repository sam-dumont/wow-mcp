# Plan your next hours

**For:** players with a wow-stats page who follow a Polestar guide. **At the end:** you have a
plan for tonight's session and you know how to change it.

The planner turns "I have two hours" into a list: which guide steps, in what order, where to
stop in town, what to look at or skip, and where to log out, at your own pace from your last sessions.
It's on your own page, in the Character tab under the character sheet.

## Make a plan

1. Pick how long you'll play: 1 to 6 hours.
2. Add what you don't want, in your own words: "no dungeons", "no flying back to town".
3. Click **Plan it**. It takes up to a minute.

<details>
<summary>What's in a plan</summary>

Time blocks, each with what to do and why:

- **The steps**: your next guide steps, with the time each one should take you.
- **Checkpoints**: the level you should have at the end of each block. Behind at one: drop the
  next optional step.
- **The town stop**: when to train, sell and repair.
- **Look at, maybe skip**: the steps that cost you more than they give at your level. Look at what holds each one up; skip it only if no later step needs it.
- **The dungeon branch**: the dungeon worth running if you find a group, and what it does to
  the rest of the plan.
- **Riding**: your gold toward it and the hours still to go.
- **Log out**: an inn or a city, so rested XP grows.

</details>

## After you play

Next time, the planner compares the last plan with what happened ("you needed 1.3× the
planned time") and scales the next one by it.

## With your AI

Ask your assistant "plan my next 2 hours, no dungeons": same plan, and you can talk it
through. The server checks every number it writes back. [A recorded
session](../../site/pages/mcp-session.md) shows one.

## When there's no plan

Without Polestar Recorder the plan goes by level and says it's an estimate: type `/psrec on`
once, then `/reload`. "No plan data yet" says what's missing, usually a level or a guide step
Polestar hasn't recorded yet: pick a guide in Polestar, play a few steps, `/reload`, and try
again in 5 minutes.
