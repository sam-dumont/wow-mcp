# Your class's meta

**For:** players with a wow-stats page. **At the end:** you know what your class and spec run
on Forever right now, how close your character is, and what to change first.

## The short version

Your character's card (the Character tab, under "your class at level N") has a **State of the
meta** box: what's strong right now for your class and spec, the build in a phrase, the stats
that matter, and the newest change to the class. Your spec is the talent tree with the most
points; with no talents on your page yet, it's the spec most players level with.

"as of Nov 20" is the last day that knowledge was checked. Past three weeks it says "may be out
of date": read it as a hint. An event for your class at your level within two weeks (a raid
opening, say) gets one more line.

Your zogzog link shows this paragraph too: it's the same for every character of that class and
spec, so it says nothing about you.

## How close you are

On your own page the box adds a score from 0 to 100: 90 and up is on the meta, 70 to 89 close,
40 to 69 partway, under 40 far from it.

**What to change** lists the gaps that cost you the most, each with what to do and where:
talent points outside the build (and the trainer who unlearns them), free points, an upgrade
and where it drops, a slot the meta enchants. An event that concerns you within four weeks
comes first: its attunement, resistance gear, consumables. **Coming up** lists what's on its
way to Forever, yours first. **The meta in full** opens the rest: every spec of your class,
talent notes for your level, stats, enchants, consumables, professions, recent changes.

<details>
<summary>How the score is counted</summary>

| Part | Counts for | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Talents | 40 | Your points that sit where the build puts them: the levelling build at your level, or at 60 for a tank or healer spec, that role's endgame build |
| Gear | 40 | Your gear's points against the best you could wear now, slot by slot (the same points as [Gear and upgrades](gear.md)) |
| Enchants | 20 | The slots the meta enchants at your level that have an enchant |

A part with nothing to measure (no talents yet, no enchant info from an out-of-date program on
your computer) is left out and the others count for more: it never counts as zero.
Consumables and professions are described, not scored. Tank and healer specs score against the
levelling build before 60 and their role's endgame build at 60; a spec with no sourced endgame
build yet shows "no target yet" and leaves the talents part out. Event advice never touches the
score.

</details>

## This week on Forever

The Overview tab of your own page has a **This week on Forever** panel: the week's news in one
line each (patches, hotfixes, class changes, openings, known issues), what concerns your
characters first. Under it, **What players are saying**: up to five of the week's busiest
threads on the official forums and r/wowforever. That's opinion, not news: nothing there sets a
date or changes your score.

Ask your AI "how close am I to the meta?" or "what's new on Forever?": it gets the same score,
every gap and what's coming ([Connect your AI](../ai/connect.md)).
