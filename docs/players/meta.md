# Your class's meta

**For:** players with a wow-stats page. **At the end:** you know what your class and spec run
on Forever right now, how close your character is, and what to change first.

## The short version

Your character's card (the Character tab, under "your class at level N") has a **State of the
meta** box: three or four sentences for your class and spec. What's strong right now, the build
in a phrase, the stats that matter, and the newest change to the class when there is one.

Your spec is the talent tree you put the most points in. With no talents on your page yet, the
box speaks about the spec most players level with.

"as of Nov 20" is the last day that knowledge was checked or changed. Past three weeks it says
"may be out of date": read it as a hint, not the rule.

When something that concerns your class at your level starts within two weeks (a raid opening,
for example), one more line says what and when.

Your friends see this paragraph on your zogzog link. It says nothing about you: it's the same
text for every character of that class and spec.

## How close am I?

On your own page (not the zogzog link) the box adds a score and what to change.

**The score**, 0 to 100, from three parts:

| Part | Counts for | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Talents | 40 | Your points that sit where the build puts them: the levelling build at your level, or at 60 for a tank or healer spec, that role's endgame build |
| Gear | 40 | Your gear's points against the best you could wear now, slot by slot |
| Enchants | 20 | The slots the meta enchants at your level that have an enchant |

A part with nothing to measure (no talents on your page yet, no enchant info from an older
shipper) is left out and the other two count for more. It never counts as zero.

- **90 and up:** on the meta.
- **70 to 89:** close.
- **40 to 69:** partway there.
- **Under 40:** far from it.

Gear points are the same Polestar points as [Gear and upgrades](gear.md): they already weigh the
stats your spec wants.

**Coming up** lists what is on its way to Forever (raid and dungeon openings, phases, new
rulesets), the ones that concern you first: when it starts, or "announced, no date" when Blizzard
hasn't given a day, and what it changes for your class.

**What to change** lists the gaps that cost you the most, in that order. When an event that
concerns you is less than four weeks away, getting ready for it comes first: an attunement (the
quest to start with, where, and what stands in the way), resistance gear, the consumables your spec
uses. Those are advice and don't touch the score. Each one says what to do and where:

- talent points outside the build: unlearn them at a class trainer (they're listed, with
  coordinates) and what it costs;
- free talent points: the next talents in the build;
- an upgrade: the item, the slot, its points and where it drops or which quest gives it;
- a slot without an enchant: the enchant the meta uses there, and who can put it on.

**The meta in full** opens the rest: how each spec of your class stands, the build's talent
notes for your level, stats, gear, enchants by slot, consumables, professions, and what changed
for the class lately, by date.

## What isn't measured

Consumables and professions are described, not scored: your page doesn't know which potions
you carry. Tank and healer specs (Protection warrior and paladin, the feral bear, Holy paladin and
priest, Discipline, Restoration druid and shaman) score against the levelling build before 60 and
against their role's endgame build at 60. A spec with no sourced endgame build yet shows "no target
yet" and the talents part is left out.

## This week on Forever

The Home tab of your own page (not the zogzog link) has a **This week on Forever** panel: the
week's news in one line each (patches, hotfixes, class changes, meta shifts, openings, known
issues, what's announced). What concerns your characters comes first, marked "for you"; an event
or a class change you can act on links to the meta box. "Earlier weeks" shows the past ones.
Under it, **What players are saying**: up to five of the week's busiest threads on the official
forums and r/wowforever, each with one line on what people are discussing and a link to the
thread. That is opinion, not news: nothing there sets a date or changes your score.

Ask your AI "how close am I to the meta?": the `class_meta` tool gives it the same score, every
gap, what's coming, and each statement with its source. "What's new on Forever?" goes to the
`news` tool ([Connect your AI](../ai/connect.md)).
