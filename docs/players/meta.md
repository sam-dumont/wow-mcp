# Your class's meta

**For:** players with a wow-stats page. **At the end:** you know what your class and spec run
on Forever right now, how close your character is, and what to change first.

## The short version

The Character tab has a **State of the meta** box: what's strong right now for your spec
(the talent tree with the most points), the build in a phrase, the stats that matter, and the
newest change to the class.

"as of Nov 20" is the last day that knowledge was checked. Past three weeks it adds "may be out
of date": read it as a hint. Your zogzog link shows this box too: it's the same for everyone of
that class and spec.

## How close you are

On your own page the box adds a score out of 100: 90 and up is on the meta, 70 to 89 close,
40 to 69 partway, under 40 far from it.

1. Read **What to change**: the gaps that cost you the most, each with what to do and where
   (talent points outside the build, free points, an upgrade, a missing enchant). An event
   coming up for you, like a raid opening, comes first with its prep.
2. Do the first one.
3. **Coming up** lists what's on its way to Forever, yours first. **The meta in full** opens
   the rest: every spec, talents, stats, enchants, consumables, professions.

<details>
<summary>How the score is counted</summary>

| Part | Counts for | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Talents | 40 | Your points that sit where the build puts them: the levelling build, or at 60 for a tank or healer, that role's endgame build |
| Gear | 40 | What you wear against the best you could wear now, slot by slot, scored like [Gear and upgrades](gear.md) |
| Enchants | 20 | The slots the meta enchants at your level that have an enchant |

A part with nothing to measure is left out, never counted as zero.

</details>

## This week on Forever

The Overview tab has a **This week on Forever** panel: the week's patches, hotfixes, class
changes and openings, one line each, yours first. **What players are saying**, under it, is
the week's busiest threads: opinion, not news.

Your AI gets the same score and gaps: ask "how close am I to the meta?" ([Connect your
AI](../ai/connect.md)).
